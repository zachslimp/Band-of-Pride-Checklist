Optional for band camp

These are suggestions, not requirements. Everything your student needs for band is on the Required items page.

Sun and heat
Hat or cap

A wide brim keeps sun out of the eyes. One that also shades the neck is even better.

Sunglasses

They help students stay focused and protect their eyes from UV rays.

Sunscreen

SPF 30 or higher, labeled broad spectrum, with enough to reapply through the day.

Water bottle

An insulated, refillable bottle works best. Students should sip all day, not wait until they feel thirsty.

Insect repellent

Only needed if bugs are a problem where camp is held.

Camp supplies
Pencils

Bring two or more. Students will write things down, and friends often forget theirs.

Extra flip folder pages

A standard flip folder comes with 5 pages, and each holds two sheets of music. Extra pages help if your student has more than 10 sheets.
